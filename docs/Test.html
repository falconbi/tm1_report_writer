CFO Commentary — Q1 FY2026

Period ending 31 March 2026 | Prepared by: Finance | Confirmed: 13 April 2026

Group revenue for Q1 FY2026 came in at $48.3M, representing +6.2% growth against prior year and -1.8% below budget. The shortfall to budget is concentrated in the ANZ Commercial segment, driven by two large contract renewals delayed into Q2. Underlying demand signals remain strong with pipeline coverage at 3.4x.

Cost discipline held firm — operating expenses of $31.7M were $0.9M favourable to budget, partly offsetting the revenue miss. EBITDA of $16.6M represents a margin of 34.4%, 80bps below the budgeted 35.2% but 120bps ahead of Q1 FY2025.

Key Takeaways

  • Revenue +6.2% YoY — growth trajectory intact despite Q1 contract timing delays
  • ANZ Commercial -$1.1M vs budget — two contracts confirmed delayed to Q2; no loss of business
  • OpEx $0.9M favourable — headcount management and discretionary spend controls effective
  • EBITDA margin 34.4% — within acceptable range; on track for full-year target of 35.0%
  • Cash position $22.4M — strong; no liquidity concerns
